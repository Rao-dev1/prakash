Key Findings

  • PRAKASH-DS successfully isolates anomalous drift from natural manufacturing process variations using non-parametric statistics (Median & MAD).
  • Separate chaining Hash Table provides guaranteed sub-microsecond component lookup ($O(1)$) across 6,000 components.
  • Max Heap Priority Queue ensures zero-overhead $O(1)$ extraction of maximum-risk components for quality engineering inspection.
  • BST In-order traversal enables deterministic, sorted serial exports without extra sorting overhead ($O(n)$).

Important Disclosures & Educational Context

  • Evaluation performed on synthetic development datasets (LOT-01 to LOT-08) with known generative parameters.
  • Designed as a decision-support and data structures educational pipeline for semiconductor burn-in screening.
  • Deterministic engineering limits take absolute precedence over statistical drift scores (failsafe design).
